# G7 active figure captions

Old figures in this directory are superseded: earlier helpers used incorrect schemas/static intervals. They are retained as historical artifacts and must not be used in the manuscript. Active assets are generated at `07_IEEE_Paper/g7/assets/` by `build_assets.py`.

Captions, denominators, stress-axis ordering and interpretation boundaries are documented in `07_IEEE_Paper/g7/supplement.md`. The letter uses architecture and stage-scoped intervals; other figures belong to the technical supplement. Source hashes are in `assets/sources.json`.
